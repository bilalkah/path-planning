## Mazes

`maze/maze.map` and `maze/maze_wide.map` are not from the benchmark set. They
are generated by `tools/maze/make_maze.py`, which lists the exact commands.
`maze.map` has corridors and walls one cell wide. `maze_wide.map` has
corridors 16 cells wide, which leaves room for RRT and RRT*.
